**Problem 1:**

Let $$F$$ be a field and suppose $$\varphi: F\rightarrow F$$ is a field automorphism.  

a) Prove that $$\varphi(0) = 1$$.

b) Prove that $$\varphi(1) = 1$$.

c) Prove that $$\varphi(m/n) = m/n$$ for all integers $$m,n\in F$$ with $$n\neq 0$$.

d) Prove that if $$a\in F$$ is a root of $$f(z) = z^3 + 7z^2-2z+5$$, then so is $$\varphi(a)$$

**Problem 2:**

Consider the field $$F = \mathbb Q(\sqrt[3]{2})$$.

a) Prove that

$$F = \text{span}_{\mathbb{Q}}\{1, \sqrt[3]{2}, \sqrt[3]{4}\}$$.

b) Prove that $$F$$ is three dimensional as a $$\mathbb Q$$-vector space.

c) Show that the only automorphism of $$F$$ is the identity map.

**Problem 3:**

  Let $$z_1 = \sqrt[3]{2}$$, $$z_2 = \sqrt[3]{2}\omega_3$$, and $$z_3 = \sqrt[3]{2}\omega_3^2$$ be the three different cube roots of two.
  Consider the field $$F = \mathbb{Q}(z_1,z_2,z_3)$$ generated by all the cube roots of two.
  We call $$F$$ the **splitting field** of $$z^3-2$$.

a) Prove that

$$F = \text{span}_{\mathbb{Q}}\{1, \sqrt[3]{2}, \sqrt[3]{4}, \omega_3, \sqrt[3]{2}\omega_3, \sqrt[3]{4}\omega_3\}$$.

b) Prove that if $$\varphi$$ is an automorphism of $$F$$, then $$\varphi$$ restricts to a permutation of $$\{z_1,z_2,z_3\}$$

c) Prove that if an automorphism $$\varphi$$ satisfies $$\varphi(z_1) = z_1$$, $$\varphi(z_2) = z_2$$ and $$\varphi(z_3) = z_3$$, then $$\varphi$$ is the identity function

**Problem 4:**

Let $$n>1$$ be an integer and consider the field $$F=\mathbb Q(\omega_n)$$.

a) Prove that

$$F = \text{span}_{\mathbb Q}\{\omega_n^k: 1\leq k < n,\ \gcd(k,n)=1\}$$

b) Prove that an automorphism $$\varphi$$ of $$F$$ must satisfy $$\varphi(\omega_n) = \omega_n^j$$ for some integer $$1\leq j < n$$ with $$\gcd(j,n) =1$$.

**Problem 5:**

Let $$z_1,z_2,$$ and $$z_3$$ be the roots of the cubic polynomial

$$z^3 + bz^2 + cz + d.$$

The **discriminant** of a cubic polynomial is the expression

$$\Delta = (z_1-z_2)^2(z_2-z_3)^2(z_3-z_1)^2.$$

a) Find an explicit expression for $$\Delta$$ in terms of $$b,c,d$$.

b) Prove that $$\Delta=0$$ if and only if there is a repeated root.

c) Prove that $$\Delta > 0$$ if and only if all the roots are real.
