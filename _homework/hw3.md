**Problem 1:**

Let $$R$$ be a commutative ring with identity and let $$S\subseteq R$$ be a subset that is closed under multiplication, ie. $$a,b\in S\Rightarrow ab\in S$$.
Define a relation $$\sim$$ on the set $$A=R\times S$$ by the rule

$$(r,s)\sim (r',s')\Longleftrightarrow rs'=r's.$$

This turns out to be an equivalence relation on $$A$$ and the set $$A/\sim$$ Of equivalence classes is called the **localization** of $$R$$ by $$S$$ and is denoted by $$S^{-1}R$$.

a) Prove that $$\sim$$ is an equivalence relation on $$A$$.

b) Prove that the binary operations $$[(r,s)]\cdot[(r',s')] = [(rr',ss')]$$ and $$[(r,s)]+[(r',s')] = [(rs'+r's,ss')]$$ are **well-defined**.

c) Prove that $$S^{-1}R$$ has the structure of a ring with identity.

d) Prove that if $$S$$ contains a zero divisor, then $$S^{-1}R$$ is the trivial ring.

**Problem 2:**

Let $$H\leq G$$ with $$[G:H] = 2$$.  Prove that $$H$$ is a normal subgroup of $$G$$.

**Problem 3:**


Suppose that $$N$$ is a normal subgroup of $$G$$ with the property that $$aba^{-1}b^{-1}\in N$$ for all $$a,b\in G$$.
Prove that $$G/H$$ is an Abelian group.


**Problem 4:**

Prove that if $$m$$ and $$n$$ are positive integers, then

$$\mathbb Z_{mn} / \langle m\rangle \cong \mathbb Z_m.$$


**Problem 5:**

Let $$F$$ be a field and $$b,c\in F$$ and consider the polynomial $$p(x) = x^2 + bx + c$$.

* (a) Prove that the set

$$I = \{p(x)f(x): f(x)\in F[x]\}$$ 

is an ideal in the ring $$F[x]$$ of polynomials with coefficients in $$F$$.

* (b) Prove that $$p(x)$$ has no roots in $$F$$, then $$I$$ is a maximal ideal.

* (c) Explicitly write down the four elements of the field $$\mathbb F_2[x]/I$$.

* (d) Write down addition, multiplication, and inversion tables for the elements of $$\mathbb F_2[x]/I$$.
